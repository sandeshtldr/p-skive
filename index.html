Rules in short

  • Set the arrow to the end of the quarter-hour you parked in: 13:07 → 13:15, 13:15 → 13:30. Rounding down gets a fine.
  • Don't touch it again until you drive off. Turning it forward is not an extension.
  • Only one disc visible, inside the windscreen, bottom right, readable from outside.
  • Parked outside the hours on the sign? You may instead set it to when the restriction starts (e.g. 08:00 for a 2-hour zone from 8–16, giving you until 10:00).
  • Hours in parentheses on a sign, like (9–14), usually apply on Saturdays.
